RESQ CELLS · PROJECT PROFILE

A decision support system, with a human in command.

ResQ Cells turns incident reports into structured needs, matches those needs to available responder capabilities, and explains a proposed response. It does not dispatch automatically.

01Structured intakeIncident reports are validated before they enter the queue.
02Deterministic allocationNearest available qualified units are assigned once, across the whole queue.
03Human authorizationAn operator must approve; unavailable capabilities block approval.
04Auditable actionsRecommendations and operator decisions are recorded locally.

Training prototype only. Not connected to emergency services. AI is optional; the default agent provider uses deterministic rules. Maps, routing, GPS, clinical triage, and external emergency feeds are simulated or unavailable.
